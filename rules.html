Rules & Policies

Short version: don't cheat, don't be toxic, and don't ruin a round for anyone else. The long version is below, and it's what staff enforce.

On this page
1. General conduct 2. Chat & voice 3. Fair play & clients 4. In-game conduct 5. Accounts & alts 6. Punishments 7. Privacy
Staff have final say. Rules can't cover everything. If something is clearly against the spirit of the server, staff can act on it even if it isn't listed here. Decisions can be appealed — see Support.

1 General conduct

  • Treat people decently. Harassment, slurs, hate speech and targeted abuse get you removed, first offence.
  • No doxxing. Never share anyone's real name, address, school, workplace or photos.
  • Keep it broadly family friendly. Assume a younger player is reading every message.
  • Don't impersonate staff, other players or content creators — skins and usernames included.
  • No advertising other servers, Discords, shops or referral links.

2 Chat & voice

  • No spam — repeated messages, caps walls, or flooding chat.
  • No filter bypassing. Creative spelling of a slur is still a slur.
  • Good games only. Trash talk is fine; being cruel to someone after a loss isn't.
  • Voice chat follows the same rules. Mic spam and soundboards get you muted.
  • Don't backseat moderate. Report it and move on — arguing in chat makes it worse.

3 Fair play & clients

Allowed: performance mods, cosmetics, keystrokes and FPS overlays, and clients like Lunar, Badlion and Feather.
Not allowed: anything that gives an advantage — reach, killaura, autoclickers, X-ray, ESP, fly, and macros that automate gameplay.
  • No cheat clients, in any game, at any time.
  • No bug abuse. Found an exploit? Report it in Discord instead of using it.
  • Staff may ask you to screenshare. Refusing or logging off mid-check counts as an admission.

4 In-game conduct

  • No teaming in solo modes — including coordinating over voice.
  • No stat boosting. Throwing games or farming wins with friends or alts isn't allowed.
  • No stalling. Hiding or dragging a round out on purpose ruins it for everyone.
  • Don't go AFK in a match. If you need to leave, leave the game.
  • No spawn trapping or anything that stops other players from being able to play.

5 Accounts & alts

  • You're responsible for your account, including anyone else you let play on it.
  • Alts are allowed, but not to dodge a punishment or boost your main.
  • Ban evasion extends the original punishment to every linked account.
  • No selling or trading accounts.

6 Punishments

  • Minor (spam, mild chat) — a warning, then a mute.
  • Moderate (teaming, stat boosting, filter bypass) — a temporary ban.
  • Severe (cheating, hate speech, doxxing, ban evasion) — a permanent ban.
  • Punishments stack. Repeat offences move up a tier.
  • Most punishments can be appealed. See ban appeals.

7 Privacy

  • What the server keeps: your Minecraft username and UUID, your IP address for ban enforcement, chat logs and game stats.
  • Why: to run games, enforce these rules and handle appeals.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • Questions or deletion requests go through a Discord ticket. Active punishment records are kept.
